**Description** (1.823 / 4.000)

```
Villa Takvimim is made for owners of holiday rental villas. Instead of notebooks, scraps of paper and endless messages, the calendars, bookings and payments of all your villas live in one place. Large text and simple screens make it easy even if you do not like fiddling with your phone.

CALENDAR AND BOOKINGS
• See the booked and free days of every villa at a glance.
• Pick check-in and check-out days and save the booking, with the guest's name, phone and party size.
• Place an option: hold dates for a guest until a set day.
• Ask "Which villa is free?" for a date range and instantly see free villas and their total prices.

PRICES AND PAYMENTS
• Enter nightly prices by month or by day; add a short-stay fee and minimum stay rules.
• Track deposits, balances and damage deposits; always know who owes what.
• Record income and expenses and see each month at a glance.
• Send the guest a booking form (PDF) with one tap.

SHARE WITH GUESTS
• Create a villa page with photos and availability, and share the link.
• Prepare a special-price offer for a guest.
• Send your free dates over WhatsApp in a few taps.

CARETAKERS AND TEAM
• Invite your caretaker to a villa with a 6-character code.
• Caretakers see the calendar and guest details; payments, income and amounts stay hidden from them.

EASY TO USE
• Guided mode shows each task step by step.
• Choose Normal, Large or Extra large text.
• Get check-in, check-out and balance reminders.
• Lock the app with Face ID or fingerprint.
• Turkish and English.

PRIVACY
Your data is stored on servers in the European Union. Guest details are never published on the villa page. No ads, no tracking. You can delete your account and all your data from the app at any time.

New accounts are approved manually for security; your account is opened shortly after you sign up.
```

## App Privacy (Gizlilik etiketi) cevapları

`ios/App/App/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` (Görev 29.5) ile bire bir aynı. Hepsinde: **kimliğe bağlı: Evet**, **izleme (tracking): Hayır**.

| App Store Connect'teki tür | Kullanım amacı | Karşılığı |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Info → Name | App Functionality | Hesap adı, rezervasyon formu |
| Contact Info → Email Address | App Functionality | Giriş |
| Contact Info → Phone Number | App Functionality | Hesap telefonu, misafir telefonu |
| User Content → Photos or Videos | App Functionality | Villa ve fiş fotoğrafları |
| User Content → Other User Content | App Functionality | Rezervasyon, fiyat, ödeme, gider kayıtları |
| Usage Data → Product Interaction | Analytics, App Functionality | Kullanım ölçümü (Görev 29.1; Hesap → Gizlilik'ten kapatılabilir) |
| Diagnostics → Other Diagnostic Data | App Functionality | Hata türü ve yeri (mesaj yok) |

- "Do you or your third-party partners use data for tracking?" → **No**.
- Toplanmayanlar: konum, kişiler (rehber), sağlık, finans bilgisi (kart), tarama / arama geçmişi, tanımlayıcılar (reklam kimliği), satın almalar (bu sürümde), hassas bilgi.
- Crash Data işaretlenmez: çökme raporu toplanmıyor; hata türü "Other Diagnostic Data".

**Abonelik açılınca (RevenueCat anahtarları girilip yeni sürüm gönderilirken):** "Purchases → Purchase History" (App Functionality, bağlı, izleme yok) hem `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`'ye hem bu etikete eklenmeli. Gizlilik metninde abonelik bölümü zaten var.
