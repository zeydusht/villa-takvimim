## Ortak kural (her isteğin başına aynen koy)
App icon for "Villa Takvimim", a villa calendar and booking app. Redraw the attached reference icon (a calendar with two binding rings and a 3x2 grid of day squares, topped by a pitched house roof with a small chimney) keeping the same drawing and composition. 1024x1024 PNG, square, full-bleed solid background, NO rounded corners (the phone adds them), NO transparency, NO text, NO gradients, NO shadows, NO outlines, NO 3D. Exactly two flat colors: background bright lime green #9FE870, symbol very dark forest green #163300. The day squares are cut out as negative space (they show the lime background). Chunky, crisp, soft geometry with slightly rounded corners, symbol fills about 62–66% of the canvas, perfectly readable at 60x60 px.

## 5 dosya — her biri ayrı istek
1. `ikon3-01.png` — Exactly the reference drawing in the two colors above (roof and calendar both #163300, day squares negative space).
2. `ikon3-02.png` — Same, but ONE day square (bottom right) is filled back in #163300 (a booked day); the other five stay negative space.
3. `ikon3-03.png` — Same as 1, slightly bolder: thicker roof, larger calendar body, 3x2 squares a bit smaller so walls are thicker; best for tiny sizes.
4. `ikon3-04.png` — Same as 1, whole symbol leaning forward about 6 degrees (dynamic, italic feel).
5. `ikon3-05.png` — Same as 1 but the grid is 2x2 larger squares instead of 3x2 (simpler).
