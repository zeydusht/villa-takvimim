# Codex'e çizdirilecek görseller

Her görseli **ayrı ayrı** iste. Aşağıdaki "Ortak stil" paragrafını her isteğin başına aynen koy, sonra o görselin tarifini ekle.
Çıkan görseli tam olarak yazan adla kaydet ve `Villa Takvimim\www\img\` klasörüne koy (klasör yoksa oluştur).
Beğenmezsen "aynı stilde tekrar çiz" de; 6 görselin hepsi aynı elden çıkmış gibi görünmeli.

## Ortak stil (her isteğe kopyala)
Flat vector illustration for a calm, friendly mobile app used by older villa owners in Turkey. Soft rounded shapes, no outlines or very thin outlines, minimal detail. Limited color palette only: deep green #0B5B57, light mint #DCEBE5, warm sand #F3E6CF, golden ochre #B0761A, soft terracotta #C8734F, and white. Plain white background, generous empty space around the subject, centered composition. Mediterranean feel (Kalkan, Turkey): white villa, turquoise pool, olive tree, bougainvillea. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no people's faces (people may appear small and simple, seen from behind or side). Square, 1024x1024, PNG.

## 1. `bos-villa.png` — ilk açılış, henüz villa yok
A small white Mediterranean villa with a flat roof and a turquoise pool, an olive tree beside it, and a small green plus sign floating above the roof, inviting the user to add their first villa.

## 2. `bos-rezervasyon.png` — villada rezervasyon yok
A simple open wall calendar with empty days, a pair of sunglasses and a beach towel resting next to it, a small sun in the corner. Calm, waiting-for-guests feeling.

## 3. `bos-foto.png` — villada fotoğraf yok
A simple camera next to three blank rounded photo frames arranged like cards, one frame showing a tiny pool outline. Suggests adding photos.

## 4. `sihirbaz-hosgeldin.png` — kurulum sihirbazı başlangıcı
A white villa with a front door slightly open, a doormat, a potted plant, and a golden key hanging in the door. Welcoming, "let's set up your villa" feeling.

## 5. `sihirbaz-hazir.png` — "Villanız hazır!"
The same white villa with the pool, now with small celebratory confetti in green, gold and terracotta, and a big green check mark in a circle above it. Joyful but calm.

## 6. `bos-takvim.png` — önümüzdeki günlerde giriş/çıkış yok
A hammock between two olive trees beside a pool, a closed small suitcase on the ground. Quiet, relaxed, "nothing scheduled" feeling.
